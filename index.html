Senior Software Engineer

June 2026 — Present

Way2News · Hyderabad

The senior work is the production path: a Kafka consumer that now holds 4× the impressions, a 40% smaller AWS bill, and a booking product rebuilt from scratch.

  • Fixed a Kafka consumer bottleneck that spiked CPU and bandwidth as daily impressions grew from 50 million to 200 million. Threshold-based batching and bulk writes replaced three MongoDB increments per event, cutting database writes ~67% and sustaining 4× traffic at a lower server cost.
  • Cut AWS costs 40%, from $10,000 to $6,000 a month, on a system serving 20 million requests a day — minification, Gzip, and bundle restructuring.
  • Rebuilt a legacy booking module as a mobile-first SPA (React 18, Vite) on an ~80-endpoint Node 24 and Express 5 API. It covers a slot calendar, multi-unit booking, approval routing, and payment collections, on PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and Socket.IO.
  • Led cross-platform push with Android and iOS developers, so booking and approval alerts arrive on the web app and the native apps.
  • Replaced a manual monthly CSV invoicing process with Zoho Billing. Finance creates and tracks invoices in the product, with automated reconciliation against delivered spend.
